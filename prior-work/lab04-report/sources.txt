- S1-S4 are SEC primary filings. Financial-statement and MD&A claims are sourced to them.
- S5 and S7 are company/management communications. Forecasts and plans are attributed to management and should be rechecked in subsequent SEC filings.
- S6 supports the reported acquisition announcement and consideration, not an assessment of value creation.
